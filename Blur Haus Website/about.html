Meet the founder

Meet Brooke.

I trained in beauty before I ever picked up a camera.

I worked behind the chair. I watched stylists I admired, talented artists doing premium work, putting out content that looked nothing like the work they were actually doing.

It bothered me because the work itself was editorial. The hands knew what they were doing. The transformations were stunning. But the feed didn't show any of that.

So I started Blur Haus Media. The studio I wish existed when I was behind the chair.

What I do is film, edit, and deliver short-form video that actually looks like the work you're doing. Editorial. On-brand. Polished without being stiff. Real without being raw.

Strategy and communication are baked into every package. We talk during shoots. You can reach me by DM or email.
