What the name means · Locked

Two halves of one identity.

The brand is built from two ideas. Every visual, copy, and creative decision should reinforce one of them.

Blur

Motion captured. The in-between moment. The cinematic energy of beauty in real motion — not posed, not staged. The atmospheric, soft-focus thing that feels real. The fleeting beauty made permanent.

Haus

A creative house. A design studio in the Bauhaus sense — intentional, modern, craft-driven. Not a freelancer for hire — a creative entity with a point of view. A space, a team, an identity.

Color Palette · Locked

Smoky. Dusty. Earthy.

A broad palette stays disciplined because every color lives in the same desaturated earth-tone family. Browns lead. Wines anchor. Burnt oranges punch. Olives and slates appear sparingly. Nothing bright. Nothing gold.

Typography · Combined System

Four typefaces.
Four jobs.

Each face has one role and never competes with another at the same level. Heavy sans labels the category. Italic serif names the specific thing. Clean sans handles body. Tracked sans handles UI.

Display · Impact Archivo Black // Categories, Brand Mark, Page Titles
cool girl
aesthetic

Archivo Black is the structural face. Heavy, dense, ALL CAPS. For the moments that label or anchor — section headers like MEMBERSHIPS or PRODUCTIONS, the brand mark itself, page titles. The scaffolding that holds everything up.

Display · Editorial Instrument Serif (italic) // Tier Names, Taglines, Soul Copy
the studio
the glimpse

Instrument Serif italic is the poetic face. For the named things — tier names like The Studio, The Glimpse, The Vignette. For taglines, pull quotes, the moments where the brand needs to feel something. Lives inside the structural categories that Archivo Black creates.

Body DM Sans · 400 / 500 // Everything Readable

DM Sans handles every paragraph, every description, every FAQ answer. Clean, modern, grown-up. Stays out of the way so the display faces can do the heavy lifting. Use weight 500 sparingly for emphasis inside running copy.

Accent Space Grotesk · 500 // UI Labels, Eyebrows, Prices
Eyebrows · Section Labels · Buttons · Prices

Space Grotesk handles the small. Tiny labels above headlines, button text, navigation, prices, captions. Tracked-out wide letterspacing. Always uppercase. Never for body. The detail face that makes everything feel considered.

Brand Voice

Cool girl. Knows her shit.

What it sounds like.

Direct. Confident. A little dry. Not chasing approval, not selling. The voice of someone who's done the work and isn't precious about it. Warm but not soft. Real but not unhinged.

Yes

Lean into.

Plants. Glow. Calm beauty products styled like still life. Warm sunlight. Espresso. Linen. Vintage mirrors. Big chunky sans on cream. Italic serif tier names. Smoky earth tones. Burnt orange button on a cream page.

No

Avoid entirely.

Gold (cut). Pink. Cursive script fonts. Sparkles, hearts, glitter. Bright color anywhere. "Babe" energy. Cutesy graphics. Anything that reads "Etsy bridal" or "MLM girlboss." Anything that tries too hard.

Reference moodboard.

Westman Atelier × Aimé Leon Dore × an art director's apartment in Tribeca. The studio of a 26-year-old who reads Cereal Magazine, has eight plants, lights candles before she shoots, and knows the difference between cream and bone.
