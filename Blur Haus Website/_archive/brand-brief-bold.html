What the name means · Locked

Two halves of one identity.

The brand is built from two ideas. Every visual, copy, and creative decision should reinforce one of them.

Blur

Motion captured. The in-between moment. The cinematic energy of beauty in real motion — not posed, not staged. The atmospheric, soft-focus thing that feels real. The fleeting beauty made permanent.

Haus

A creative house. A design studio in the Bauhaus sense — intentional, modern, craft-driven. Not a freelancer for hire — a creative entity with a point of view. A space, a team, an identity.

Color Palette · Locked

Smoky. Dusty. Earthy.

A broad palette is fine because every color stays in the same desaturated earth-tone family. Browns lead. Wines anchor. Burnt oranges punch. Olives and slates appear sparingly. Nothing bright. Nothing saturated. Nothing gold.

Typography · Bold Sans Direction

One heavy sans.
One clean sans.
One sharp accent.

Three fonts. Editorial through weight contrast — big chunky black headlines against quiet body copy. The "ABOUT ME" magazine-cover energy from your moodboard. All three are free on Google Fonts.

Display Archivo Black · Heavy
cool girl
aesthetic
Headlines hit hard.

Archivo Black is the chunky, dense, ALL-CAPS-ready display face. For hero copy, section headers, the moments where the brand needs presence. Uppercase reads like a magazine cover. Sentence case reads like Aimé Leon Dore.

Body DM Sans · 400 / 500

DM Sans stays as the workhorse. Clean, modern, grown-up. Reads well at any size. The contrast between Archivo Black headers and DM Sans body is what gives this direction its editorial weight — heavy display, quiet body.

Use weight 500 when you need emphasis inside running copy. Bold is rare. The display does the heavy lifting.

Accent Space Grotesk · 500
Eyebrows · Section Labels · UI

Space Grotesk stays as the third voice — slightly nerdy modern energy. Tiny details: section labels, eyebrow tags above headlines, button text, navigation, prices. Tracked-out wide letterspacing. Always uppercase. Never for body.

Brand Voice

Cool girl. Knows her shit.
Doesn't try too hard.

What it sounds like

Direct. Confident. A little dry. Not chasing approval, not selling. The voice of someone who's done the work and isn't precious about it. Warm but not soft. Real but not unhinged.

Yes

Lean Into.

Plants. Glow. Calm beauty products styled like still life. Warm sunlight. Espresso. Linen. Vintage mirrors. A messy desk that still looks intentional. Big chunky sans on cream. Smoky earth tones. Burnt orange button on a cream page.

No

Avoid.

Gold (cut). Pink (not your lane). Cursive script fonts. Sparkles, hearts, glitter. Bright color anywhere. "Babe" energy. Cutesy graphics. Anything that reads "Etsy bridal" or "MLM girlboss." Anything that tries too hard.

Reference

Aimé Leon Dore × Ami Colé × that "ABOUT ME" magazine cover from your Pinterest. Heavy sans. Generous space. Earth tones. The studio of a 26-year-old who reads Cereal Magazine, has eight plants, and knows the difference between cream and bone.
