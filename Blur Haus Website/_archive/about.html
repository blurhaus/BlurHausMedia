The Origin

I'm Brooke. I'm a cosmetologist.

I trained in beauty before I ever picked up a camera. I worked behind the chair. I watched stylists I admired — talented, real artists doing premium work — put out content that looked nothing like the work they were actually doing. Phone footage. Bad lighting. No story.

It bothered me. Because the work itself was editorial. The hands knew what they were doing. The transformations were stunning. But the feed didn't show any of that.

So I started Blur Haus Media. The studio I wish existed when I was behind the chair.

What I Do

Production. Not management.

I'm not a social media manager. I don't post for you. I don't write your captions. I don't run your ads or manage your DMs. There are people who do that, and they're great at it.

What I do is film, edit, and deliver short-form video that actually looks like the work you're doing. Editorial. On-brand. Polished without being stiff. Real without being raw.

You post. You build the relationships. I make sure the content is worth posting.

What I Believe

Three things I won't compromise on.

01

Quality over quantity.

I'd rather deliver six reels you'll actually post than twenty you'll scroll past. Every shoot is intentional. Every edit is considered.

02

Your brand, not mine.

Every shoot is built around you — your voice, your service, your moment. Not a template. Not a trend. The content has to look like the work you actually do.

03

Real relationships.

Strategy and communication are baked into every package. We talk during shoots. You can reach me by DM or email. No formal calendar required.
