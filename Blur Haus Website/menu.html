About ad spend

Your ad budget is separate from our fee and you pay Meta directly, from your own account. You own the account, the data, and the audiences. Below $1,000 a month Meta doesn't get enough data to optimize, which is why that's the floor — if the budget isn't there yet, a membership below is the better first step.
